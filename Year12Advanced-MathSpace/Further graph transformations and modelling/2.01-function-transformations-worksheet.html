1 · Match parameters

For y=kf(a(x-b))+c, match each effect to k,a,b,c.
1 Horizontal translation · 2 Vertical translation · 3 Vertical dilation/reflection in the x-axis · 4 Horizontal dilation/reflection in the y-axis

Letters with x are horizontal; letters outside the function are vertical.

  • 1 → C (b)
  • 2 → D (c)
  • 3 → A (k)
  • 4 → B (a)

Verified against the 2.01 answer key.

2 · True or false

a The graph of y=f(x+2) is y=f(x) shifted 2 units right.

b y=-f(x) is a reflection of y=f(x) in the y-axis.

c y=f(3x) is a horizontal compression of y=f(x) by a factor of 1/3.

d A vertical shift up by 4 changes y-coordinates and leaves x-coordinates unchanged.

  • a False. y=f(x-b) with b=-2 is a shift left of 2. Rewrite as f(x-(-2)).
  • b False. Multiplying the output by -1 reflects in the x-axis. Reflection in the y-axis is y=f(-x).
  • c True. Here a=3, so the horizontal factor is 1/|a|=1/3 (narrower graph).
  • d True. (x,y)\mapsto(x,y+4).

Verified against the 2.01 answer key (F, F, T, T).

3 · Equations from words · f(x)=x^3

a Vertical translation 5 down · b Horizontal translation 1 left · c Reflection in the x-axis then vertical stretch 2 · d Reflection in the y-axis
  • a y=x^3-5
  • b Left 1 means b=-1, so y=(x+1)^3
  • c k=-2 (stretch 2 and reflect): y=-2x^3
  • d y=(-x)^3=-x^3

Verified against the 2.01 answer key (the key also notes (-x)^3=-x^3).

Parent key points (-4,0),(-2,-2),(0,0),(2,2),(4,0). Sketch separately: a y=f(x)+2 · b y=f(x-2) · c y=f(x-2)+2.

5 · Sketch dilations

Parent key points (-2,-2),(-1,0),(0,2),(1,0),(2,-2). a y=3f(x) · b y=f(2x) · c y=f(-x/2).

parent (question)

Use (x,y)\to(x/a,\,ky).

a k=3: (-2,-6),(-1,0),(0,6),(1,0),(2,-6)

b a=2 (compress by 1/2): (-1,-2),(-0.5,0),(0,2),(0.5,0),(1,-2)

c a=-1/2 (stretch by 2 and reflect in y-axis): (4,-2),(2,0),(0,2),(-2,0),(-4,-2)

6 · Describe the sequence

a x^2 to (x-4)^2+6 · b |x| to -|x+1|-3 · c \sqrt{x} to 3\sqrt{x}-2 · d 1/x to 1/(2x)+5
  • a Horizontal shift 4 right, vertical shift 6 up.
  • b Reflection in the x-axis, horizontal shift 1 left, vertical shift 3 down.
  • c Vertical stretch by 3, vertical shift 2 down.
  • d Horizontal compression by 1/2 (because 1/(2x)=f(2x)), vertical shift 5 up. Equivalent: vertical compression by 1/2 and the same vertical shift, since 1/(2x)=\frac12\cdot\frac1x.

Verified against the 2.01 answer key (including the equivalent form in d).

7 · Image of P(3,-6)

a y=f(x)+4 · b y=f(x-9) · c y=\frac13 f(x) · d y=f(-3x) · e y=-f(x/2)+1 · f y=f(2x+4)-3

Rule: (x,y)\mapsto\bigl(x/a+b,\,ky+c\bigr) for y=kf(a(x-b))+c.

  • a (3,-2) — add 4 to y.
  • b (12,-6) — add 9 to x.
  • c (3,-2) — multiply y by 1/3.
  • d a=-3 so x'=3/(-3)=-1: (-1,-6).
  • e a=1/2, k=-1, c=1 → (6,7).
  • f Factor: 2x+4=2(x+2) so a=2, b=-2, c=-3 → (-0.5,-9).

Verified against the 2.01 answer key.

8 · Write the equation (correct order)

a \sqrt{x} reflected in the y-axis, vertical dilation 4, up 3.
b x^2 dilated horizontally with scale factor 1/3, 2 right, 5 down.
c |x| translated 4 left, then horizontal dilation scale factor 2, then reflected in the x-axis.
  • a y=4\sqrt{-x}+3
  • b Scale factor 1/3 means a=3, so (3x)^2=9x^2, then shift: y=9(x-2)^2-5
  • c Left 4 gives |x+4|. Horizontal factor 2 means a=1/2, so |x/2+4|. Then k=-1: y=-\left|\dfrac{x}{2}+4\right|

Verified against the 2.01 answer key.

9 · One mapping (2,4)\to(2,-8)

Identify a transformation sending (2,4) on y=f(x) to (2,-8).

The x-coordinate is unchanged, so a purely vertical map is enough. -8= -2\times 4, so a vertical stretch of 2 and a reflection in the x-axis works: y=-2f(x).

Also valid: 4-12=-8, so y=f(x)-12.

Verified against the 2.01 answer key (“answers may vary”; both examples above are listed there).

10 · Point on y=3f(2(x-1))-5

(4,2) lies on y=f(x). Find the image on the transformed graph.

k=3, a=2, b=1, c=-5.

(4,2)\mapsto\bigl(4/2+1,\,3\cdot2-5\bigr)=(3,1).

In words: horizontal compression by 1/2, shift 1 right, vertical stretch 3, shift 5 down.

Verified against the 2.01 answer key (point (3,1)).

11 · Which order is correct?

x^2 to y=2x^2+4. Student A: stretch 2 then up 4. Student B: up 4 then stretch 2. Who is right?

Track (2,4) on y=x^2.

  • A: stretch 2 → (2,8); up 4 → (2,12), which lies on y=2x^2+4.
  • B: up 4 → (2,8); stretch 2 → (2,16), which does not.

Algebra: A is 2x^2+4. B is 2(x^2+4)=2x^2+8.

Verified against the 2.01 answer key (Student A).

12 · Equation of the solid graph

The solid graph is a transformation of y=|x| (dashed). Find its equation.

y=|x|solid graph

Vertex of |x| at (0,0) has moved to (1,3) → shift 1 right and 3 up, form y=a|x-1|+3.

The graph opens downwards, so a<0. Use the visible point (2,1):

1=a|2-1|+3\Rightarrow a+3=1\Rightarrow a=-2.

Equation: y=-2|x-1|+3.

Verified against the 2.01 answer key.

13 · Factor before you shift

A common mistake with y=f(3x+6) is to call this a shift of 6 left. Factor and explain.

3x+6=3(x+2), so y=f\big(3(x+2)\big): horizontal compression by 1/3, then shift 2 left.

A shift of 6 left would be f(x+6). Compressing that gives f(3(x+6))=f(3x+18), which is not f(3x+6).

Verified against the 2.01 answer key.

14 · Triangle and area

Vertices A(0,0), B(4,0), C(2,6) on y=f(x). a Area of ABC. b y=2f(x): new vertices and area. c y=f(0.5x): new vertices and area. d How do the two dilations affect area?

a Base 4, height 6: area \frac12\times4\times6=12.

b (x,y)\to(x,2y): A'(0,0), B'(4,0), C'(2,12). Area 24.

c (x,y)\to(x/0.5,y)=(2x,y): A'(0,0), B'(8,0), C'(4,6). Area 24.

d Vertical dilation by |k| multiplies area by |k|. Horizontal dilation by 1/|a| multiplies area by 1/|a|.

Verified against the 2.01 answer key.
