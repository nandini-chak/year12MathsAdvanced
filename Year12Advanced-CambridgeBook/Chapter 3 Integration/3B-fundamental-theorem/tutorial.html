Theory · key ideas

Evaluate a definite integral using a primitive — the fundamental theorem.

  • If F'=f, then \displaystyle\int_a^b f=F(b)-F(a).
  • Find any primitive → substitute upper − lower.
  • Do not integrate across a discontinuity.
  • This links area to differentiation.
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a)
