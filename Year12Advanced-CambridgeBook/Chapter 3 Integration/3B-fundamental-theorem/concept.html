3B · The fundamental theorem of calculus

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

The big shortcut: evaluate a definite integral using a primitive.

  • Idea: if F'=f, then \displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a).
  • Steps: find any primitive → substitute upper − lower.
  • Warning: do not integrate across a break/discontinuity.
  • Why “fundamental”: it links area (integral) to derivatives (primitives).

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
