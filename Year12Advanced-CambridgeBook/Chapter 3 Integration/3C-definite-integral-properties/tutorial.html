Theory · key ideas

Rules for negatives, flipping limits, odds/evens, and splitting.

  • Region below the axis contributes a negative signed amount.
  • \int_b^a=-\int_a^b; \int_a^a=0.
  • Odd/even symmetry can cut work on [-a,a].
  • Integrals add and scale like ordinary sums.
