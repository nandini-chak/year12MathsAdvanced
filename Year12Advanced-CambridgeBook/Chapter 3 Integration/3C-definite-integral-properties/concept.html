3C · The definite integral and its properties

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Rules for splitting, flipping limits, negatives, odds/evens, and inequalities.

  • Below the axis: that region contributes a negative amount to the integral.
  • Flip limits: \int_b^a=-\int_a^b; zero-width: \int_a^a=0.
  • Odd / even: symmetry can kill half the work on symmetric intervals.
  • Sums & multiples: integrals split and scale like ordinary sums.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
