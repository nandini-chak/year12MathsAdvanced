3A · Areas and the definite integral

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

The definite integral measures signed area under a curve between two x-values.

  • Picture it: lots of thin rectangles under y=f(x) — add their areas — take a limit.
  • Need: f continuous on the closed interval.
  • Notation: \displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.
  • Warm-up: some integrals match known shapes (triangles, semicircles).

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. For full teaching detail, open the Tutorial (or the earlier section where you first met the idea).

  • Signed area below the axis is negative.
