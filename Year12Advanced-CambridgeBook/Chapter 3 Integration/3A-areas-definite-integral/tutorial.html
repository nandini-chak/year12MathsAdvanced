Theory · key ideas

A definite integral is the (signed) area under a curve between two x-values.

  • Picture thin rectangles → add → take a limit.
  • Need continuity on the closed interval.
  • Notation: \displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.
  • Some integrals match known shapes (triangle, semicircle).
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx
