What this section is about

The indefinite integral is the family of all primitives — write +C.

  • Notation: \displaystyle\int f(x)\,dx=F(x)+C where F'=f.
  • Same as: “find the general primitive”.
  • Check: differentiate your answer — you should get the integrand back.
