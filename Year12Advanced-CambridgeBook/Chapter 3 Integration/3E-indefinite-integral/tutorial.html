Theory · key ideas

The indefinite integral is the family of all primitives — remember +C.

  • \displaystyle\int f(x)\,dx=F(x)+C with F'=f.
  • Same skill as finding primitives (§2I).
  • Check by differentiating your answer.
  • Next: reverse chain rule and more forms.
