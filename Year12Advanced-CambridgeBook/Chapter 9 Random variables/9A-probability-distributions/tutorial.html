Theory · key ideas

A discrete distribution lists values and probabilities that sum to 1.

  • Each P(x)\ge 0 and \sum P(x)=1.
  • Show as a table or probability histogram.
  • Event probabilities: add the matching outcomes.
  • Random variable = numerical outcome of a chance experiment.
\sum P(x)=1
