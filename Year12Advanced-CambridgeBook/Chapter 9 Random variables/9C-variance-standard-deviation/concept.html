9C · Variance and standard deviation

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Variance and SD measure how spread out the values are around the mean.

  • Variance: average squared distance from the mean (weighted by probability).
  • SD: square root of variance — same units as X.
  • Shortcut: often \mathrm{Var}(X)=E(X^2)-[E(X)]^2.
  • Scale: know how aX+b changes variance/SD.
