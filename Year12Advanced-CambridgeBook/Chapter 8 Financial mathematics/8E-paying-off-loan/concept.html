8E · Paying off a loan

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Loans with regular repayments — balance shrinks each period.

  • Each period: interest added, then repayment subtracted.
  • Model: recurrence / GP-style sum for the outstanding balance.
  • Typical asks: repayment size · time to pay off · final payment.
  • Check: balance should reach (about) zero at the end.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
