Theory · key ideas

Loans: interest added, then a regular repayment subtracted.

  • Model the outstanding balance step by step or with a closed form.
  • Typical asks: repayment size, time to clear, final payment.
  • Balance should reach about zero at the end.
  • Watch the order: interest then repayment (as worded).

More practice lives on the Exercise page. This tutorial stays short on purpose (Very Poor knowledge level).
