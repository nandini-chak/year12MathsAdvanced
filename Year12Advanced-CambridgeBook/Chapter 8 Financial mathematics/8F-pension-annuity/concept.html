8F · The pension from a super fund or annuity

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Drawing a regular pension/annuity from a fund that still earns interest.

  • Picture it: reverse of saving — withdrawals instead of deposits.
  • Model: similar GP/sum structure with a different sign pattern.
  • Asks: how long the fund lasts · how much you can withdraw.
  • Care: timing of interest vs withdrawal in the wording.
