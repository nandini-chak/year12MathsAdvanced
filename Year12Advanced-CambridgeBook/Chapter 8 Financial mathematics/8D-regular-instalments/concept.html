8D · Investing money by regular instalments

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Saving by paying the same amount regularly into an account that earns interest.

  • Picture it: each deposit grows for a different number of periods.
  • Model: usually a GP of growing deposits — then sum the series.
  • Goal: final balance after n payments.
  • Care: does interest apply before/after each deposit? Read the wording.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
