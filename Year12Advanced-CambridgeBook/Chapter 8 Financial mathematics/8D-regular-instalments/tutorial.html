Theory · key ideas

Regular deposits into an interest-earning account.

  • Each deposit grows for a different number of periods.
  • Usually a GP of growing deposits — then sum.
  • Read whether interest applies before/after each deposit.
  • Goal: final balance after n payments.

More practice lives on the Exercise page. This tutorial stays short on purpose (Very Poor knowledge level).
