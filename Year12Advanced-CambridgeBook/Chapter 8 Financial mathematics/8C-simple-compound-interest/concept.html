8C · Simple and compound interest

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Compare simple interest (linear) with compound interest (exponential / GP).

  • Simple: interest each year on the original principal only.
  • Compound: interest earns interest — multiply by (1+r) each period.
  • Formulae: learn which letters mean principal, rate, time, amount.
  • We practise: find amount, rate, or time.
