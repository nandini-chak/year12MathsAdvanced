Theory · key ideas

Simple interest vs compound interest.

  • Simple: interest on the original principal only (linear).
  • Compound: interest earns interest — multiply by (1+r) each period.
  • Learn which letters mean principal, rate, time, amount.
  • Compound links to GPs.
A=P(1+r)^n\quad\text{(compound, per period)}
