1F · Summing an arithmetic series

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Add the first n terms of an AP with ready-made formulae.

  • Trick: write the sum forward and backward — pairs all equal a+\ell.
  • Formula: S_n=\dfrac n2(a+\ell) (first + last).
  • Or: S_n=\dfrac n2\big(2a+(n-1)d\big) when you know a and d.
  • We practise: choose the formula that matches the given information.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. For full teaching detail, open the Tutorial (or the earlier section where you first met the idea).

  • AP sum: S_n=\tfrac n2(2a+(n-1)d)=\tfrac n2(a+\ell).
  • Know both forms.
