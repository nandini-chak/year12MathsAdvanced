Tutorial · theory

1F · Summing an arithmetic series

Two short formulae add the first n terms of an AP. Use the one that matches the numbers you already know.

T Teacher note
Each formula sits on its own row. Each example states the question, then substitutes one number at a time. Prefer S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}(a+a_n) when the last term is known.

Theory · key ideas

An AP adds the same gap each time. The sum of the first n terms is S_n. You do not have to add the terms one by one.

  • First and last known. Write the sum forwards and backwards. Each pair adds to first + last. There are n pairs, so twice the sum is n times that pair. Divide by 2.
  • First term and gap known. The last term is a+(n-1)d. Put that into the first formula and tidy.
  • Which one. If you can see the last term, use the first formula. If you know the gap d and how many terms, use the second.
  • Count the terms. From 100 to 200 inclusive is 200-100+1=101 terms, not 100.
S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}(a+a_n)
S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}\big(2a+(n-1)d\big)

Worked mini-examples

Example · first term and last term

Question. Add the integers from 100 to 200 inclusive.

Formula: S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}(a+a_n). Here a is the first term, a_n is the last term, and n is how many terms you are adding.

Step 1 · First term a=100. Last term a_n=200. The gap is 1.

Step 2 · How many terms: n=200-100+1=101.

Step 3 · S_{101}=\dfrac{101}{2}(100+200).

Step 4 · 100+200=300.

Step 5 · \dfrac{101}{2}\times 300=101\times 150=15150.

Answer · The sum is 15 150.

Example · first term and common difference

Question. The series is 100+94+88+82+\cdots. Find S_{10} and S_{41}.

Formula: S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}\big(2a+(n-1)d\big). Here a is the first term and d is the gap. The (n-1) means you have made n-1 jumps to reach term n.

Step 1 · a=100. d=94-100=-6.

Step 2 · For 10 terms, n-1=9. S_{10}=\dfrac{10}{2}\big(2\times 100+9\times(-6)\big).

Step 3 · 2\times 100=200. 9\times(-6)=-54. So the bracket is 200-54=146.

Step 4 · 5\times 146=730. So S_{10}=730.

Step 5 · For 41 terms, n-1=40. S_{41}=\dfrac{41}{2}\big(200+40\times(-6)\big).

Step 6 · 40\times(-6)=-240. The bracket is 200-240=-40.

Step 7 · \dfrac{41}{2}\times(-40)=41\times(-20)=-820.

Answer · S_{10}=730 and S_{41}=-820.

Example · find how many terms, then add

Question. How many terms are in 41+45+49+\cdots+401? Then find the sum.

Formula: The nth term is a_n=a+(n-1)d. Once you know n and the last term, use S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}(a+a_n).

Step 1 · a=41 and d=45-41=4. The last term is 401.

Step 2 · 41+(n-1)\times 4=401.

Step 3 · (n-1)\times 4=360.

Step 4 · n-1=90, so n=91.

Step 5 · S_{91}=\dfrac{91}{2}(41+401).

Step 6 · 41+401=442. \dfrac{91}{2}\times 442=91\times 221=20111.

Answer · There are 91 terms, and the sum is 20 111.
