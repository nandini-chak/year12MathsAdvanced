a Start with 0.1. Each term is 2 times the previous term. The sequence is: 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, . . . Hence, this is a GP with a = 0.1 and r = 2. a101 is equivalent to the thickness from 100 successive folds. an = arn−1 a101 = 0.1 × 2100 a101 = 2100 10 mm ≑1.27 × 1023 km ≑1.34 × 1010 light years Note that the universe is estimated to be 13.8 billion years old. So the paper thickness is about the distance light would travel in the age of the universe. To answer tricky follow up questions from curious students: Light that reaches us today from the early universe (the CMB, as far back as we can see) was originally emitted "only" 42 million light-years away, when everything was a condensed plasma. While space expanded, that light had to travel for 13.8 billion years to reach us. That original piece of the plasma that we’re seeing now would currently be about 46 billion light-years away, due to the expansion of space. a Explain whether or not the infinite sequence 7, 7, 7, . . . is a GP. b What function corresponds to this sequence, and can it be written as an exponential function?

a Yes, it is a GP. It has first term a = 7 and common ratio r = 7 7 = 1. b Since each term is the same, the constant function y = 7 represents this sequence i.e. f(x) = 7 for all input x. Further, as 1x = 1 for all x, y = 7 × 1x.
