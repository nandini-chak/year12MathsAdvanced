Theory · key ideas

A GP multiplies by the same number each step — the common ratio r.

  • Test: check \dfrac{a_2}{a_1}, \dfrac{a_3}{a_2}, … are all equal to r (terms ≠ 0).
  • Recursive: a_n=a_{n-1}\times r.
  • nth term: a_n=ar^{n-1}.
  • Link: like exponential growth/decay sampled at whole numbers.
a_n=ar^{n-1}
