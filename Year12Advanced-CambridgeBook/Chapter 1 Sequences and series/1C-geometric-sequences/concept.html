1C · Geometric sequences

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

A geometric sequence (GP) multiplies by the same number each time.

  • Picture it: 2,6,18,54,\ldots — each term is 3 times the one before.
  • Common ratio r: \dfrac{a_n}{a_{n-1}}=r (never zero).
  • nth term: a_n=ar^{n-1}.
  • Link: like exponential growth/decay when you plot the terms.
  • We practise: finding a, r, or a_n, and GP word problems.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. For full teaching detail, open the Tutorial (or the earlier section where you first met the idea).

  • Geometric: common ratio r.
  • a_n=ar^{{n-1}}.
  • Sign of r controls growth / oscillation.
