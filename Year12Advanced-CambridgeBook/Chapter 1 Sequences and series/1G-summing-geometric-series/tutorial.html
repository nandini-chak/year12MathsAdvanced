Tutorial · theory

1G · Summing a geometric series

A GP is added by multiplying the sum by the ratio and subtracting. Most of the terms cancel.

T Teacher note
Use a(r^n-1)/(r-1) when r>1, and a(1-r^n)/(1-r) when r<1. When r=1 the fraction formula does not work: every term equals a, so S_n=na.

Theory · key ideas

A GP multiplies by the same number r each time. The sum of the first n terms is S_n.

  • The trick. Write S_n. Multiply every term by r. Subtract. The middle terms cancel, and two ends are left.
  • When r>1. S_n=a\dfrac{r^n-1}{r-1}.
  • When r<1. The same formula, written the tidy way: S_n=a\dfrac{1-r^n}{1-r}. Use this for a negative ratio as well.
  • When r=1. Every term is a, so S_n=na. The fraction would divide by zero.
  • Count the powers. From 5^0 to 5^7 is 8 terms, because you count both ends.
S_n=a\dfrac{r^n-1}{r-1}\qquad(r>1)
S_n=a\dfrac{1-r^n}{1-r}\qquad(r<1)
S_n=na\qquad(r=1)

Worked mini-examples

Example · a ratio bigger than 1

Question. Add every power of 5 from 5^0 to 5^7.

Formula: S_n=a\dfrac{r^n-1}{r-1} when r>1. Here a is the first term, r is the multiplier, and n is how many terms.

Step 1 · The terms are 1,\;5,\;25,\;\ldots,\;5^7. So a=1 and r=5.

Step 2 · Powers 0 through 7 are 8 terms. n=8.

Step 3 · S_8=\dfrac{5^8-1}{5-1}.

Step 4 · 5^8=390625, so the top is 390624.

Step 5 · 390624\div 4=97656.

Answer · The sum is 97 656.

Example · a negative ratio

Question. Find the sum of the first six terms of 2-6+18-\cdots.

Formula: S_n=a\dfrac{1-r^n}{1-r} when r<1. A negative ratio is less than 1.

Step 1 · a=2. r=(-6)\div 2=-3.

Step 2 · S_6=2\times\dfrac{1-(-3)^6}{1-(-3)}.

Step 3 · (-3)^6=729, because the power is even. Top: 1-729=-728.

Step 4 · Bottom: 1-(-3)=4.

Step 5 · 2\times\dfrac{-728}{4}=2\times(-182)=-364.

Answer · S_6=-364.

Example · the sum is given, find the first term

Question. The sum of the first four terms of a GP with ratio 3 is 200. Find the four terms.

Formula: S_n=a\dfrac{r^n-1}{r-1}. You know S_4, r and n. The unknown is a.

Step 1 · a\dfrac{3^4-1}{3-1}=200.

Step 2 · 3^4=81, so the top is 80. The bottom is 2.

Step 3 · a\times\dfrac{80}{2}=200, so 40a=200.

Step 4 · a=5.

Step 5 · Multiply by 3 each time: 5, 15, 45, 135.

Answer · The four terms are 5, 15, 45 and 135.
