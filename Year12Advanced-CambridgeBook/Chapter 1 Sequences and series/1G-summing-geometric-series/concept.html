1G · Summing a geometric series

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Add the first n terms of a GP (different method from APs).

  • Method: write S_n, multiply by r, subtract — most terms cancel.
  • Formula: S_n=a\dfrac{r^n-1}{r-1} when r\neq 1.
  • If |r|<1, often use S_n=a\dfrac{1-r^n}{1-r} (same idea, tidier).
  • Special case: if r=1, every term is a so S_n=na.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
