Theory · key ideas

A series means we add the terms. S_n is the sum of the first n terms.

  • S_n=a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_n (partial sum).
  • Sigma notation \sum is a compact “add these” symbol.
  • Sequence = list; series = the total of that list.
  • Later sections give closed formulae for AP and GP sums.
S_n=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_k

More practice lives on the Exercise page. This tutorial stays short on purpose (Very Poor knowledge level).
