1E · Adding up the terms of a sequence

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

A series means we add the terms of a sequence.

  • Partial sum S_n: add the first n terms — S_n=a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_n.
  • Picture it: falling distances 5+15+25+\cdots in 10 seconds — that total is a sum.
  • Sigma notation: \sum is a compact way to write “add these terms”.
  • Word tip: sequence = list; series = the sum of that list.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
