Theory · key ideas

A sequence is an ordered list with a first term, then a next, then a next.

  • Finite stops (has a last term); infinite never stops.
  • Repeats are allowed (unlike people in a line).
  • We write a_n for the nth term (a_1 = first).
  • Specify by: listing · formula for a_n · recursive rule (next from previous).
a_1,\;a_2,\;a_3,\;\ldots

More practice lives on the Exercise page. This tutorial stays short on purpose (Very Poor knowledge level).

Worked mini-examples

Example · reading a list

Step 1 · In 5,8,11,14, we have a_1=5, a_2=8, a_4=14.

Step 2 · It looks like “add 3 each time” — that idea becomes an AP in §1B.

Example · formula for a_n

Step 1 · If a_n=2n+1, then a_1=3, a_2=5, a_3=7 (odd numbers).

Step 2 · Substitute the term number into the formula — that is all.
