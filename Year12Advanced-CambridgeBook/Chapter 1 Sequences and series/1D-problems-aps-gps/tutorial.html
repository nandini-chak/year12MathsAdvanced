Theory · key ideas

Mix AP and GP tools — especially three-term tests.

  • Three numbers a,m,b in AP ⇔ m=\tfrac12(a+b).
  • Three non-zero numbers a,g,b in GP ⇔ g^2=ab.
  • Write the formula that matches the story, then solve the equation.
  • Always check: equal differences (AP) or equal ratios (GP).
m=\tfrac12(a+b)\quad\text{(AP)}\qquad g^2=ab\quad\text{(GP)}
