1D · Solving problems involving APs and GPs

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Use AP and GP formulae together to solve mixed problems.

  • Three in AP: middle = average of the other two — m=\tfrac12(a+b).
  • Three in GP: middle squared = product — g^2=ab.
  • Strategy: decide AP or GP, write the formula, solve for the unknown.
  • Check: plug answers back — differences equal (AP) or ratios equal (GP).
