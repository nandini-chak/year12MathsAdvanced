1I · Recurring decimals and geometric series

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

A recurring decimal is an infinite GP — so we can turn it into a fraction.

  • Idea: 0.\dot3=0.333\ldots=\dfrac{3}{10}+\dfrac{3}{100}+\dfrac{3}{1000}+\cdots (a GP).
  • Method: write the GP, use S_\infty=\dfrac{a}{1-r}, simplify.
  • Goal: every recurring decimal ↔ a fraction (rational number).
  • We practise: one-digit and multi-digit repeats.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
