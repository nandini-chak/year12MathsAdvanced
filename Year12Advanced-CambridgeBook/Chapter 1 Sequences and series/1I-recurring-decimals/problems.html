Quick fact: Recurring decimals convert to fractions using a GP for the repeating tail, then S_\infty.

a · a2 −a1 = 83 −76 = 7 a3 −a2 = 90 −83 = 7 Hence there is a common difference between the terms, so it is an AP with common difference of 7.
