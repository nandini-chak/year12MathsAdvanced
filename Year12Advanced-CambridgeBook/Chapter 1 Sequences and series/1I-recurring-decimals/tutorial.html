Theory · key ideas

A recurring decimal is an infinite GP — convert it to a fraction with the limiting-sum formula.

  • Write the repeating part as a GP of place values.
  • Use S_\infty=\dfrac{a}{1-r} with |r|<1.
  • Simplify to a fraction in lowest terms.
  • Every recurring decimal is rational.
0.\dot{d}=\dfrac{d}{9},\;0.\dot{d_1d_2}=\dfrac{d_1d_2}{99},\;\ldots

More practice lives on the Exercise page. This tutorial stays short on purpose (Very Poor knowledge level).

Worked mini-examples

Example · 0.\dot3

Step 1 · 0.333\ldots=\dfrac{3}{10}+\dfrac{3}{100}+\dfrac{3}{1000}+\cdots with a=\tfrac3{10}, r=\tfrac1{10}.

Step 2 · S_\infty=\dfrac{3/10}{1-1/10}=\dfrac13.
