48 Chapter 1 Sequences and series 1I 1I Recurring decimals and geometric series Learning intentions • Interpret a recurring decimal as a GP with a limiting sum. • Hence convert a recurring decimal to a fraction. Every rational number can be written as a fraction, so can be written as terminating or recurring decimal — use long division if necessary. Conversely, every recurring decimal can be written as a fraction, but this procedure has not been so straightforward. Now, however, we can express a recurring decimal as an infinite GP — its value is the limiting sum of that GP, which is easily expressed as a fraction.
