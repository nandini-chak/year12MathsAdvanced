1H · The limiting sum of a geometric series

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Some infinite GPs have a limiting sum — the totals get as close as we like to a fixed number.

  • Picture it: frog jumps 4,2,1,\tfrac12,\ldots metres — totals approach 8 (water!).
  • Condition: need |r|<1 so later terms shrink toward 0.
  • Formula: S_\infty=\dfrac{a}{1-r} when |r|<1.
  • If |r|\ge 1: no finite limiting sum (terms do not die away).

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. For full teaching detail, open the Tutorial (or the earlier section where you first met the idea).

  • If |r|<1, limiting sum S_\infty=\dfrac{{a}}{{1-r}}.
  • Otherwise the infinite sum does not settle.
