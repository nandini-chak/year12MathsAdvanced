17 · Exercise 1H

The climate is changing in Azakan. A few years ago, the annual rainfall was 400 mm. The next year it rose 20%, and the next year it fell 20%, and the years have continued in this sequence. If this pattern continues, how much rain will ’eventually’ have fallen in Azakan, taking the year of 400 mm rainfall as the first year?

Consider one period consisting of 2 years, whereupon annual rainfall will increase and decrease accord- ingly. Hence, the rainfall forms a GP with common ratio r = 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96. In the first period (2 years, starting at 400mm), 400 + 400(1.2) = 880mm fell i.e. let a = 880. Since r < 1, a limiting sum exists and the total rainfall that will eventually have fallen is: S ∞= a 1 −r = 880 1 −0.96 = 22 000mm
