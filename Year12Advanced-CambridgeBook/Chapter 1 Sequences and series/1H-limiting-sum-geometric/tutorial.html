Theory · key ideas

An infinite GP can have a limiting sum when later terms shrink away.

  • Need |r|<1 so r^n\to 0.
  • Then S_\infty=\dfrac{a}{1-r}.
  • If |r|\ge 1, there is no finite limiting sum.
  • Picture: frog jumps 4+2+1+\tfrac12+\cdots → total approaches 8.
S_\infty=\dfrac{a}{1-r}\quad(|r|<1)
