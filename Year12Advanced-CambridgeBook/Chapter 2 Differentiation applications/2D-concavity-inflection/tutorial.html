Theory · key ideas

Concavity is the bend; inflection is where the bend flips.

  • f''>0 → concave up (smiley).
  • f''<0 → concave down (frown).
  • Inflection: concavity changes (often f''=0 with a sign change).
  • Second-derivative test can classify some stationary points.
