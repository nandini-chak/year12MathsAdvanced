Theory · key ideas

A checklist to sketch systematically with calculus.

  • Domain → intercepts → asymptotes → stationary points → concavity → join.
  • Sign of f can flip only at a zero or a discontinuity.
  • Use f' for hills/valleys; f'' for bend.
  • Label key points clearly on the final sketch.
