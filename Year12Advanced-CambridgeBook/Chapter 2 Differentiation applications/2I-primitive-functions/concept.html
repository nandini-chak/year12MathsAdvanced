2I · Primitive functions

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

A primitive of f is any function whose derivative is f — reverse of differentiating.

  • Picture it: x^2, x^2+3, x^2-6 all have derivative 2x.
  • +C: primitives differ by a constant (the family of parallel curves).
  • Initial condition: one known point pins down C.
  • Why: this is the toolkit for integration in Chapter 3.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
