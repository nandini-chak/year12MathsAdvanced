Theory · key ideas

A primitive of f is any F with F'=f — reverse differentiating.

  • Primitives differ by a constant: write +C.
  • An initial condition pins down C.
  • Standard power rule reversed: raise the power by 1, divide by the new power.
  • This is the toolkit for Chapter 3 integration.
\int x^n\,dx=\dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C\quad(n\neq -1)
