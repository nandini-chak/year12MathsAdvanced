Question · a.

In the diagram, name the points where the derivative has the sign asked for. B, D, F and H sit where the curve is momentarily flat.

(a) f'(x)>0

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · A positive derivative means the curve is climbing as we move to the right.

Step 2 · From the left, the curve climbs through A. After the trough at F it climbs through G, and after the flat point H it climbs through I.

Step 3 · C and E are on downhill stretches, so they are not in this list. B, D, F and H are flat, so the derivative is zero there, not positive.

A, G and I.

Question · b.

In the diagram, name the points where the derivative has the sign asked for. B, D, F and H sit where the curve is momentarily flat.

(b) f'(x)<0

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · A negative derivative means the curve is going down as we move to the right.

Step 2 · After the crest at B the curve falls through C. After the flat point D it falls through E toward F.

C and E.

Question · c.

In the diagram, name the points where the derivative has the sign asked for. B, D, F and H sit where the curve is momentarily flat.

(c) f'(x)=0

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · A zero derivative means the tangent is horizontal.

Step 2 · The curve levels off at the crest B, at the flat point D, at the trough F, and at the flat point H.

Question · a.

Find the derivative. Then substitute x=1 and say whether the curve is increasing, decreasing or stationary there.

(a) y=x^2

Formula: Power rule: the derivative of x^n is nx^{n-1}. A constant disappears.

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · The derivative of x^2 is 2x. The power 2 comes down, and the new power is 1.

Step 2 · Put x=1 into the derivative. y'=2\times 1=2

Step 3 · 2>0, so read the sign.

Question · a.

Explain each statement from the sign of the derivative.

(a) y=-5x+2 is decreasing for every x

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · The derivative of -5x is -5, and the constant 2 disappears. So y'=-5 for every x.

Step 2 · -5<0 everywhere, so the line always slopes down.

Decreasing for all x.

Question · b.

Explain each statement from the sign of the derivative.

(b) y=x+7 is increasing for every x

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · The derivative of x is 1. So y'=1 for every x.

Step 2 · 1>0 everywhere, so the line always slopes up.

Increasing for all x.

Question · c.

Explain each statement from the sign of the derivative.

(c) f(x)=x^3 is increasing except at x=0, where it is stationary

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · The derivative is f'(x)=3x^2. A square is never negative.

Step 2 · When x\neq 0, 3x^2>0. When x=0, 3\times 0=0.

Increasing for every x except x=0, where it is stationary.

Question · d.

Explain each statement from the sign of the derivative.

(d) f(x)=x^2 is increasing for x>0 and decreasing for x<0. What happens at x=0?

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · The derivative is f'(x)=2x.

Step 2 · If x<0, then 2x<0. If x>0, then 2x>0. If x=0, then 2x=0.

Decreasing for x<0, stationary at x=0, increasing for x>0.

Quick fact: f'(x)>0 means the curve is going up (increasing). f'(x)<0 means it is going down (decreasing). f'(x)=0 means the tangent is horizontal (stationary).

Step 1 · Let u=x and v=x+1. Then u'=1 and v'=1, so f'(x)=((x+1)-x)/(x+1)^2=1/(x+1)^2.

Step 2 · Substitute. f'(2)=1/3^2=1/9

Step 3 · 1/9>0

Increasing at x=2.
