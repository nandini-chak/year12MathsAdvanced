Theory · key ideas

The sign of f'(a) tells you the local behaviour at x=a.

  • f'(a)>0 → increasing (tangent slopes up).
  • f'(a)<0 → decreasing (tangent slopes down).
  • f'(a)=0 → stationary (flat tangent).
  • Always say “at the point” — this is local language.
f'(a)>0\Rightarrow\text{inc.};\quad f'(a)<0\Rightarrow\text{dec.};\quad f'(a)=0\Rightarrow\text{stat.}
