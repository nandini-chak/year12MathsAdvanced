2A · Increasing, decreasing, and stationary at a point

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Use the derivative’s sign to say how a curve behaves at a point.

  • Increasing at x=a: slope up — f'(a)>0.
  • Decreasing at x=a: slope down — f'(a)<0.
  • Stationary at x=a: flat tangent — f'(a)=0.
  • Picture it: think “is the tangent climbing, falling, or level?”

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. For full teaching detail, open the Tutorial (or the earlier section where you first met the idea).

  • f\prime(x)>0 increasing; f\prime(x)<0 decreasing.
  • Stationary: f\prime(x)=0.
