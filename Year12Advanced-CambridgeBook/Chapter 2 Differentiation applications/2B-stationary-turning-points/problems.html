19 · Exercise 2B Challenge

(a) The derivative of an even function is odd. Draw even graphs and explain why.

(b) The derivative of an odd function is even. Draw odd graphs and explain why.

(c) Explain how this shows up when you differentiate powers of x.

Question · a.

Why is the derivative of an even function odd?

Quick fact: Even means f(-x)=f(x): the graph is a mirror in the y-axis. Odd means f(-x)=-f(x).

Step 1 · On an even graph, the slope a distance h to the right of the axis is the opposite of the slope the same distance to the left.

Step 2 · A parabola y=x^2 climbs on the right and falls on the left at matching steepness. So f'(-x)=-f'(x).

The derivative graph is odd.

Question · b.

Why is the derivative of an odd function even?

Step 1 · An odd graph, such as y=x^3, has the same kind of slope on the left and on the right: both sides climb.

Step 2 · The steepness at -h equals the steepness at h, so f'(-x)=f'(x).

The derivative graph is even.

Question · c.

What happens for powers of x?

Step 1 · An even power, such as x^4, is an even function. Its derivative 4x^3 has an odd power.

Step 2 · An odd power, such as x^5, is an odd function. Its derivative 5x^4 has an even power.

Step 3 · Differentiating lowers the power by 1, which swaps even and odd.

Even powers differentiate to odd powers, and odd powers differentiate to even powers.
