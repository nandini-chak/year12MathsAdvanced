To answer this question as framed, it is best to graph polynomial functions that have only even powers of x. Derivatives of such polynomial functions will therefore have only odd powers of x. So f(−x) = f(x) and f ′(−x) = −f ′(x). To formally prove that the derivative of an even function is odd, we would need to prove that if f is even then f ′(−x) = −f ′(x).
