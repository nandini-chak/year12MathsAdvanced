Theory · key ideas

Classify each stationary point using a slope table.

  • Stationary ⇔ f'(x)=0.
  • Max turning: slope + then − (hill).
  • Min turning: slope − then + (valley).
  • Horizontal inflection: flat, but slope does not change sign.
