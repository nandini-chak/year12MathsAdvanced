2B · Stationary points and turning points

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Classify each flat spot: turning point or horizontal inflection.

  • Stationary: f'(x)=0 (tangent horizontal).
  • Max turning: slope changes + to − (hill top).
  • Min turning: slope changes − to + (valley).
  • Horizontal inflection: flat, but slope does not change sign.
  • Tool: a table of slopes left and right of the zero of f'.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
