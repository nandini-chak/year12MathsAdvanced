Theory and ideas

2C Second and higher derivatives 67 2C Second and higher derivatives Learning intentions • Review second and higher derivatives. The derivative of the derivative of a function is called the second derivative of the function. There are various notations, including: d2y dx2 and f ′′(x) and f (2)(x) and y′′ and y(2). This short section reviews higher derivatives from Section 9D of the Year 11 book in preparation for the geometric use of the second derivative in the next section. Example 10 Finding second and higher derivatives of a function Find the successive derivatives of y = x4 + x3 + x2 + x + 1. Solution y = x4 + x3 + x2 + x + 1 d2y dx2 = 12x2 + 6x + 2 d4y dx4 = 24 dy dx = 4x3 + 3x2 + 2x + 1 d3y dx3 = 24x + 6 d5y dx5 = 0 Because the fifth derivative is zero, all the higher derivatives are also zero.
