What this section is about

• Review second and higher derivatives. The derivative of the derivative of a function is called the second derivative of the function. Example 10 Finding second and higher derivatives of a function Find the successive derivatives of y = x4 + x3 + x2 + x + 1. Solution y = x4 + x3 + x2 + x + 1 d2y dx2 = 12x2 + 6x + 2 d4y dx4 = 24 dy dx = 4x3 + 3x2 + 2x + 1 d3y dx3 = 24x + 6 d5y dx5 = 0 Because the fifth derivative is zero, all the higher derivatives are also zero.
