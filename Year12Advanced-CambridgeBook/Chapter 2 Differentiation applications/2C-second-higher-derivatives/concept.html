What this section is about

• Review second and higher derivatives. The derivative of the derivative of a function is called the second derivative of the function. There are various notations, including: d2y dx2 and f ′′(x) and f (2)(x) and y′′ and y(2). This short section reviews higher derivatives from Section 9D of the Year 11 book in preparation for the geometric use of the second derivative in the next section.
