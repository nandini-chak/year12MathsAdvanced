Example 4 Using the chain rule with linear functions as exponents Use the chain rule, with full setting out, to differentiate: y = e3x+4 a y = eax+b b Solution a Let y = e3x+4. Then using the chain rule, dy dx = dy du × du dx = 3 e3x+4. Let u = 3x + 4. Then y = eu. Hence du dx = 3 and dy du = eu (the key property of ex). b Let y = eax+b. Then using the chain rule, dy dx = dy du × du dx = a eax+b. Let u = ax + b. Then y = eu. Hence du dx = a and dy du = eu (the key property of ex). Using an extended standard form Part b above gives us an extended standard form: d dx eax+b = aeax+b .
