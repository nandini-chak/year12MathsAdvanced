4B · Differentiation involving exponential functions

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Differentiate functions built from e^{\ldots}.

  • Core: \dfrac{d}{dx}(e^x)=e^x.
  • Chain: \dfrac{d}{dx}\big(e^{f(x)}\big)=e^{f(x)}f'(x).
  • Also: product / quotient with exponentials.
  • We practise: tidy forms before differentiating.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
