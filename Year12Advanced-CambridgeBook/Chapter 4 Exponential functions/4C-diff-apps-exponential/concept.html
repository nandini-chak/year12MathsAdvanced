4C · Applications of differentiation (exponential)

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Apply exponential derivatives to rates, tangents, and models.

  • Typical: gradient at a point · increasing/decreasing · max/min of a model.
  • Context: growth, decay, cooling, or similar word problems.
  • Method: differentiate → interpret the number in the story.
  • Units: keep meaning attached (people per year, °C per hour, …).
