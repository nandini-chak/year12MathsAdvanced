Theory · key ideas

Use exponential derivatives in rates, tangents, and models.

  • Differentiate the model → interpret the number.
  • Typical: gradient, growth rate, max/min of a quantity.
  • Keep units (people/year, °C/h, …).
  • Check the answer makes sense in the story.
