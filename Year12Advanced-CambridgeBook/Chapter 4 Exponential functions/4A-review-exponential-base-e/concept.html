We defined the number e ≑2.7183 to be the base so that the exponential graph y = ex has gradient exactly 1 at its y-intercept. It is an irrational number, and plays a role in exponential functions very similar to the role of π in trigonometric functions. x y 1 −1 1 2 e The most significant properties of y = ex and its graph are listed below in Box 1: 1 The function y = ex • There is only one exponential function y = ex that is its own derivative, and the number e ≑2.7183 is defined to be the base of this function. Thus: d dx ex = ex , that is, at each point, gradient equals height. • The gradient at the y-intercept is 1 (where the height is also 1). • Differentiating again, d2 d2x ex = ex, so the function is always concave up. • Thus the function is always positive, it is always increasing, and the rate of increase is always increasing. • The domain is all real numbers, and the range is y > 0. • The line y = 0 is a horizontal asymptote on the left, and y →∞as x →∞.
