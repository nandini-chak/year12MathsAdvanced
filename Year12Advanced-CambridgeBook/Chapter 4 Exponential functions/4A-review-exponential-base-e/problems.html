1 · Index laws Foundation

Simplify using the index laws.

a 2^3\times 2^7 · b e^4\times e^3 · c 2^6\div 2^2 · d e^8\div e^5 · e (2^3)^4 · f (e^5)^6

  • a 2^{10}
  • b e^7
  • c 2^4
  • d e^3
  • e 2^{12}
  • f e^{30}

2 · Index laws with e^{kx} Foundation

Simplify.

a e^{2x}\times e^{5x} · b e^{10x}\div e^{8x} · c (e^{2x})^5 · d e^{2x}\times e^{-7x} · e e^x\div e^{-4x} · f (e^{-3x})^4

  • a e^{7x}
  • b e^{2x}
  • c e^{10x}
  • d e^{-5x}
  • e e^{5x}
  • f e^{-12x}

3 · Approximate powers of e Foundation

Write as a power of e, then approximate correct to 4 s.f.

a e^2 · b e^{-3} · c e · d 1/e · e \sqrt{e} · f 1/\sqrt{e}

  • a e^2\approx 7.389
  • b e^{-3}\approx 0.04979
  • c e^1\approx 2.718
  • d e^{-1}\approx 0.3679
  • e e^{1/2}\approx 1.649
  • f e^{-1/2}\approx 0.6065

4 · Derivatives and shape Foundation

a Write down the first and second derivatives of y=e^x.

b Complete: “The curve y=e^x is always concave … and is always …, and the rate of … is … .”

  • a y'=e^x, y''=e^x
  • b Always concave up, always increasing, and the rate of increase is increasing.

5 · Tangents to y=e^x Foundation

a At P(1,e): find the gradient, the tangent equation, and show the x-intercept is 0.

b At Q(0,1): find the tangent and show the x-intercept is -1.

c At R(-1,1/e): find the tangent and show the x-intercept is -2.

  • a Gradient e; tangent y=ex; when y=0, x=0.
  • b Gradient 1; tangent y=x+1; x-intercept -1.
  • c Gradient 1/e; tangent y=\dfrac{1}{e}(x+2); x-intercept -2.

6 · Tangent and normal Foundation

On y=e^x-1 at x=1:

a Find the y-coordinate of P.

b Find dy/dx and its value at x=1.

c Find the tangent and normal at P in general form.

  • a P=(1,e-1)
  • b dy/dx=e^x; at x=1, gradient e
  • c Tangent: e^x-y-1=0 (or y=ex-1). Normal: x+ey-e^2+e-1=0

7 · Single transforms of e^x Foundation

Sketch each curve using one transformation of y=e^x, and describe it.

a y=e^x+1 · b y=e^x-2 · c y=\dfrac13 e^x · d y=e^{x/2}

  • a Shift e^x up 1
  • b Shift e^x down 2
  • c Stretch e^x vertically with factor \tfrac13
  • d Stretch e^x horizontally with factor 2

8 · Transforms of e^{-x} Foundation

Sketch each using one transformation of y=e^{-x}.

a y=e^{-x}-1 · b y=-e^{-x} · c y=e^{-2x}

  • a Shift e^{-x} down 1
  • b Reflect e^{-x} in the x-axis
  • c Stretch e^{-x} horizontally with factor \tfrac12

9 · Read a dilation from a graph Development

The graph (book diagram) is a dilation of y=e^x. Describe the dilation and write the equation.

Vertical dilation of y=e^x with factor -\dfrac13. Equation: y=-\dfrac13 e^x.

10 · Expand and simplify Development

a (e^x+1)(e^x-1) · b (e^{4x}+3)(e^{2x}+3) · c (e^{-3x}-2)e^{3x} · d (e^{-2x}+e^{2x})^2

  • a e^{2x}-1
  • b e^{6x}+3e^{4x}+3e^{2x}+9
  • c 1-2e^{3x}
  • d e^{-4x}+2+e^{4x}

11 · Sum of powers of e Development

Write as a sum of powers of e.

a \dfrac{e^{4x}+e^{3x}}{e^{2x}} · b \dfrac{e^{2x}-e^{3x}}{e^{4x}} · c \dfrac{e^{10x}+5e^{20x}}{e^{-10x}} · d \dfrac{6e^{-x}+9e^{-2x}}{3e^{3x}}

  • a e^{2x}+e^x
  • b e^{-2x}-e^{-x}
  • c e^{20x}+5e^{30x}
  • d 2e^{-4x}+3e^{-5x}

12 · Growth and decay at the intercept Development

a Gradient of the tangent to y=e^x at its y-intercept?

b What transformation maps y=e^x to y=e^{-x}?

c Use that to find the gradient of y=e^{-x} at its y-intercept.

d Sketch both on one axes set.

e How can the transformation be read as a dilation?

  • a Gradient 1 (at (0,1))
  • b Reflection in the y-axis
  • c Gradient -1
  • d Both through (0,1); one growth, one decay
  • e Horizontal dilation with factor -1

13 · Higher derivatives Development

Write the first four derivatives. For which curves does gradient equal height at every point?

a y=e^x+5 · b y=e^x+x^3 · c y=4e^x · d y=5e^x+5x^2

  • a All four derivatives are e^x (gradient ≠ height)
  • b e^x+3x^2, e^x+6x, e^x+6, e^x
  • c All four are 4e^x — here gradient equals height
  • d 5e^x+10x, 5e^x+10, 5e^x, 5e^x

14 · Angles of inclination Development

For the tangent to y=e^x, find the gradient and angle of inclination (nearest minute) at:

a x=0 · b x=1 · c x=-2 · d x=5

Draw a diagram of the curve and the four tangents.

  • a Gradient 1; angle 45^\circ
  • b Gradient e; angle \approx 69^\circ 48'
  • c Gradient e^{-2}; angle \approx 7^\circ 42'
  • d Gradient e^5; angle \approx 89^\circ 37'

15 · Tangent only Development

On y=e^x-1 at x=1: find P, dy/dx, and the tangent equation.

  • a P=(1,e-1)
  • b dy/dx=e^x; at x=1, value e
  • c Tangent y=ex-1

16 · Sequence of transforms Challenge

Describe each step and sketch.

a Dilation: y=e^{2x}

b Then translation: y=e^{2(x-1)}

c Then dilation: y=\dfrac12 e^{2(x-1)}

d Then translation: y=\dfrac12 e^{2(x-1)}-2

  • a Stretch horizontally with factor \tfrac12
  • b Shift right by 1
  • c Stretch vertically with factor \tfrac12
  • d Shift down by 2

17 · Two readings of the same transform Challenge

a From y=e^x to y=e^{x+2}: as a translation, then as a dilation.

b From y=e^x to y=2e^x: as a dilation, then as a translation (write 2 as e^{\log_e 2}).

  • a Translation: shift left 2. Dilation: vertical factor e^2 (since e^{x+2}=e^2 e^x).
  • b Dilation: vertical factor 2. Translation: y=e^{x+\log_e 2} — shift left by \log_e 2.
