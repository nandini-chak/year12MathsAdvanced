4D · Integration involving exponential functions

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Integrate exponential functions (reverse of Section 4B).

  • Core: \displaystyle\int e^x\,dx=e^x+C.
  • Chain style: \displaystyle\int e^{ax+b}\,dx=\dfrac1a e^{ax+b}+C (a\neq 0).
  • Definite: use the fundamental theorem as usual.
  • Check: differentiate to confirm.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.

Keep these in mind. For full teaching detail, open the Tutorial (or the earlier section where you first met the idea).

  • Reverse chain when the inner derivative appears.
