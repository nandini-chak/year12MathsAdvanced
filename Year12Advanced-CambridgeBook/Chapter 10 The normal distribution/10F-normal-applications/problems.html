Method 1 (Algebraic): P(X > 100) = 97.7, so transforming to standard normal: P Z > 100 −µ σ ! = 97.7 P Z < µ −100 σ ! = 97.7 µ −100 σ = 2 (by reading the tables in reverse) µ = 100 + 2σ P(X < 115) = 69.1, so transforming to standard normal: P Z < 115 −µ σ ! = 69.1 115 −µ σ = 0.5 (by reading the tables in reverse) 115 −µ = 0.5σ µ = 115 −0.5σ Solving these equations simultaneously, we have µ + 4µ = 100 + 460, hence µ = 112. Method 2 (Intuitive approach): Using the standard normal tables in reverse, data above 2σ below the mean corresponds to the probability P(X > 100) = 97.7. Data below 0.5σ above the mean corresponds to the probability P(X < 115) = 69.1. Thus 2.5 standard deviations is 115 −100 = 15 g, so 1 standard deviation is 6 g. The mean weight is 112 g (half a standard deviation below 115 g).
