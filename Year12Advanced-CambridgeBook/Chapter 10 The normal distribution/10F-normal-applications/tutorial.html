Theory · key ideas

Apply the normal model to everyday measurements.

  • Identify \mu and \sigma → standardise → find probability or cut-off.
  • Wording: “at least”, “between”, “top 5%” change the inequality.
  • Sketch the bell and shade the region.
  • Many real data sets are only approximately normal.
