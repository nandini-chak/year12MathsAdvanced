Theory · key ideas

Cumulative totals answer “up to here”; grouping helps continuous data.

  • Add frequencies (or probabilities) from the left.
  • Cumulative graphs rise (or stay flat) — never go down.
  • Class intervals group continuous measurements for histograms.
  • Prepares CDF language for continuous distributions.
