10A · Cumulative frequency and grouping

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Cumulative frequency / probability builds “up to here” totals; grouping helps continuous data.

  • Cumulative: add frequencies (or probabilities) from the left.
  • Picture it: “how many / what chance is ≤ this value?”
  • Grouping: continuous data often needs class intervals for histograms.
  • Why: prepares CDF ideas for continuous distributions.
