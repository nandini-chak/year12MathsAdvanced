Theory · key ideas

Any normal is a shift/stretch of the standard normal — use z-scores.

  • General: mean \mu, SD \sigma.
  • Standardise: z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}.
  • Then use standard normal probabilities.
  • Empirical rule scales with \sigma.
z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}
