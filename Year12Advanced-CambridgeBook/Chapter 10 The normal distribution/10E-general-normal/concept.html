10E · General normal distributions

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Any normal is a stretch and shift of the standard normal — use z-scores.

  • General: mean \mu, standard deviation \sigma.
  • Standardise: z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}.
  • Then: use standard normal probabilities.
  • Also: empirical rule and quartiles after scaling.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
