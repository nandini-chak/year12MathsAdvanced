Theory · key ideas

Standard normal: bell curve with mean 0 and SD 1.

  • PDF \phi(z); CDF \Phi(z) = area to the left.
  • Empirical rule ≈ 68% / 95% / 99.7% within 1 / 2 / 3 SDs.
  • Use tables or calculator for probabilities.
  • Inflections near z=\pm 1.
Z\sim N(0,1)
