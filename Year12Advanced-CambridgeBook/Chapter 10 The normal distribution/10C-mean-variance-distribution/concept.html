What this section is about

Mean and variance for continuous distributions — sums become integrals.

  • Mean: E(X)=\displaystyle\int x f(x)\,dx (over the domain).
  • Variance: same idea as discrete, with integrals instead of sums.
  • Analogy: Greek \Sigma ↔ integral sign \int.
  • We practise: set limits carefully from the PDF’s support.
