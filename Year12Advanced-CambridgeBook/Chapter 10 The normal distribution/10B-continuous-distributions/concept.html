10B · Continuous distributions

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Continuous random variables use areas under a density curve — not probabilities at single points.

  • PDF f(x): curve whose area = probability.
  • CDF F(x)=P(X\le x): running total from the left.
  • Link: F'=f (where smooth); integrate PDF to get CDF/probabilities.
  • Single point: P(X=a)=0 for a continuous variable.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
