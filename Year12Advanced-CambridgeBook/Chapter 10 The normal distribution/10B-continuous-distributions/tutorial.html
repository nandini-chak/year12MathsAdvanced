Theory · key ideas

Continuous variables use areas under a density curve.

  • PDF f: area = probability; single points have probability 0.
  • CDF F(x)=P(X\le x).
  • Where smooth: F'=f; integrate PDF to get probabilities.
  • Domain may be a finite interval or unbounded.
P(a\le X\le b)=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx
