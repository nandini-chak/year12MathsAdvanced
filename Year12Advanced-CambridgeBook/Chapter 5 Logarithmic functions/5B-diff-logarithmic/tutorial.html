Theory · key ideas

Differentiate logs — especially natural log.

  • (\ln x)'=\dfrac1x for x>0.
  • Chain: (\ln f)'=\dfrac{f'}{f} when f>0.
  • Log laws can simplify before differentiating.
  • Stay inside the domain of the log.
\dfrac{d}{dx}\ln f(x)=\dfrac{f'(x)}{f(x)}
