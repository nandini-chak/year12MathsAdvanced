5B · Differentiation involving logarithmic functions

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Differentiate logarithmic functions.

  • Core: \dfrac{d}{dx}(\ln x)=\dfrac1x (x>0).
  • Chain: \dfrac{d}{dx}\big(\ln f(x)\big)=\dfrac{f'(x)}{f(x)} (when f>0).
  • Tip: log laws can make differentiating easier before you start.
  • Also: product / quotient mixes with other functions.
