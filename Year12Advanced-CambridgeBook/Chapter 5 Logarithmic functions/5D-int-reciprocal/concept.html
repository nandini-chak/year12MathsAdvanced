What this section is about

Integrals that produce logarithms — especially \int \dfrac1x\,dx.

  • Core: \displaystyle\int \dfrac1x\,dx=\ln|x|+C.
  • Why absolute value: works for negative x too (where defined).
  • Forms: \int\dfrac{f'}{f} often becomes \ln|f|.
  • We practise: rewrite the integrand until you see that pattern.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
