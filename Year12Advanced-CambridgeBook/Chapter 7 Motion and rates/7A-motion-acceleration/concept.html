What this section is about

Motion review: displacement, velocity, acceleration linked by derivatives.

  • Chain: x(t) → v=x' → a=v'=x''.
  • Signs: positive/negative direction on a number line or axis.
  • Stationary: v=0; turning often when velocity changes sign.
  • We practise: reading motion from equations and simple graphs.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
