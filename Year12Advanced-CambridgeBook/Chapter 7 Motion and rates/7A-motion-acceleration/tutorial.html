Theory · key ideas

Motion chain: position → velocity → acceleration by differentiating.

  • v=x', a=v'=x''.
  • Sign = direction on the axis.
  • Stationary when v=0.
  • Read equations and simple motion graphs carefully.
v=\dfrac{dx}{dt},\quad a=\dfrac{dv}{dt}
