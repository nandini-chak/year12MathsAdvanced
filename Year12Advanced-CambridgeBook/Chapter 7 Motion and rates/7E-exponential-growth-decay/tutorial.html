Theory · key ideas

Rate proportional to amount → exponential solutions.

  • \dfrac{dQ}{dt}=kQ → Q=Ae^{kt}.
  • k>0 growth; k<0 decay.
  • Fit A and k from data.
  • Half-life / doubling time are special cases.
