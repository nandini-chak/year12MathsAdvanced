7E · Exponential growth and decay

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Models where the rate of change is proportional to the current amount.

  • Form: \dfrac{dQ}{dt}=kQ → solutions like Q=Ae^{kt}.
  • Growth: k>0; decay: k<0.
  • Half-life / doubling: special times that double or halve the amount.
  • We practise: fit constants from data, then predict.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
