7C · Rates and differentiation

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise → next section. No extra hubs.

What this section is about

Related rates: how fast one quantity changes when another does.

  • Idea: relate quantities by a formula, then differentiate both sides w.r.t. time.
  • Steps: draw → equation → differentiate → substitute known rates.
  • Units: e.g. cm/s, L/min — keep them consistent.
  • Typical: expanding circle, sliding ladder, filling tank.

Full detail and examples live in the Tutorial · theory — this page only sets the scene.
