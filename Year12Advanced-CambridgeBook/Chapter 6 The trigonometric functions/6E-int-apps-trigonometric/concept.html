What this section is about

• Find areas associated with graphs of trigonometric functions. The trigonometric integrals can now be used to find areas in the usual way. Finding areas by integration As always, a sketch is essential, because an area below the x-axis is represented as a negative number by the definite integral. It is best to evaluate the separate integrals first, then make a conclusion about areas.
