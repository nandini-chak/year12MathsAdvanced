Theory · key ideas

Differentiate sine, cosine, tangent (radians).

  • (\sin x)'=\cos x, (\cos x)'=-\sin x, (\tan x)'=\sec^2 x.
  • Chain: multiply by the inside derivative.
  • Product/quotient when mixed with other functions.
  • Calculus forms assume radians.
(\sin nx)'=n\cos nx
