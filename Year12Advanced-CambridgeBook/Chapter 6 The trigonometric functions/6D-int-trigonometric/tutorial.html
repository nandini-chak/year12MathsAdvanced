Theory · key ideas

Integrate basic trig functions.

  • \int\sin x=-\cos x+C, \int\cos x=\sin x+C.
  • Also \int\sec^2 x=\tan x+C.
  • For \sin(nx), divide by n after integrating.
  • Check by differentiating.
\int\sin(nx)\,dx=-\dfrac1n\cos(nx)+C
