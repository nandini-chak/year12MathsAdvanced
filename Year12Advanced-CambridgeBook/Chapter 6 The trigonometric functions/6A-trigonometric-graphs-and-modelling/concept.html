What this section is about

We take the familiar waves y=\sin x, y=\cos x and y=\tan x and transform them — stretch, compress, shift left/right, shift up/down — then use those graphs to model springs, tides and temperature.

y=A\sin\big(n(x-b)\big)+c
  • A controls height (amplitude for sine/cosine).
  • n controls how fast the wave repeats (period).
  • b shifts the graph horizontally.
  • c shifts the mean position vertically.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. For full teaching detail, open the Tutorial (or the earlier section where you first met the idea).

  • Radians: full turn 2\pi; landmarks 0,\pi/2,\pi,3\pi/2,2\pi.
  • Parents: \sin x,\cos x period 2\pi, range [-1,1]; \tan x period \pi.
  • Transform language: f(x-b) horizontal, f(x)+c vertical, Af stretch, f(nx) period change.
