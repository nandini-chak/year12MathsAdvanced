1 · Amplitude of k sin x Foundation

a Sketch each graph for 0\le x\le 2\pi, stating the amplitude.

i y=\tfrac12\sin x · ii y=2\sin x · iii y=3\sin x

b Describe the transformation from y=\sin x to y=k\sin x (k>0).

c How does the amplitude change as k increases?

  • a i amplitude \tfrac12 · ii amplitude 2 · iii amplitude 3
  • b Vertical stretch by factor k.
  • c Amplitude increases (the wave becomes taller).

i y=\tfrac12\sin x

ii y=2\sin x

iii y=3\sin x

2 · Period of cos nx Foundation

a Sketch for 0\le x\le 2\pi and state the period.

i y=\cos\tfrac12 x · ii y=\cos 2x · iii y=\cos 3x

b Describe the transformation from y=\cos x to y=\cos nx (n>0).

c How does the period change as n increases?

  • a i period 4\pi · ii period \pi · iii period 2\pi/3
  • b Horizontal stretch by factor 1/n.
  • c Period decreases as n increases.

i y=\cos\tfrac12 x

ii y=\cos 2x

iii y=\cos 3x

3 · Period of tan ax Foundation

a Sketch for 0\le x\le 2\pi and state the period.

i y=\tan x · ii y=\tan\tfrac12 x · iii y=\tan 2x

b Describe the transformation from y=\tan x to y=\tan ax (a>0).

c How does the period change as a increases?

  • a i period \pi · ii period 2\pi · iii period \pi/2
  • b Horizontal stretch by factor 1/a.
  • c Period decreases as a increases.

Dashed verticals mark asymptotes.

i y=\tan x

ii y=\tan\tfrac12 x

iii y=\tan 2x

4 · Horizontal shifts of sine Foundation

a Sketch for 0\le x\le 2\pi.

i y=\sin(x+\pi/2) · ii y=\sin(x+\pi) · iii y=\sin(x+2\pi)

b Describe the transformation from y=\sin x to y=\sin(x+\alpha) (\alpha>0).

c What happens when \alpha is a multiple of 2\pi?

  • a i equals \cos x · ii equals -\sin x · iii same as \sin x
  • b Shift left by \alpha.
  • c Unchanged (full-period shift).

i y=\sin(x+\pi/2)

ii y=\sin(x+\pi)

iii y=\sin(x+2\pi)

5 · Vertical shifts of cosine Foundation

a Sketch for 0\le x\le 2\pi; state mean value and range.

i y=\cos x+1 · ii y=\cos x+2 · iii y=\cos x+\tfrac12

b Describe the transformation from y=\cos x to y=\cos x+c (c>0).

c How does the graph change as c increases?

  • a i mean 1, range [0,2] · ii mean 2, range [1,3] · iii mean 1/2, range [-1/2,3/2]
  • b Vertical shift up by c.
  • c The graph (and mean) move up.

i y=\cos x+1

ii y=\cos x+2

iii y=\cos x+\tfrac12

  • a Shift asymptotes and graph \pi/2 right.

8 · Sequence from sin x Development

Write a sequence of transformations from y=\sin x, then sketch for 0\le x\le 2\pi.

a y=3\sin 3x · b y=-2\sin\tfrac12 x · c y=3\sin(x-\pi/2)+2

  • a Horizontal stretch factor 1/3, then vertical stretch factor 3.
  • b Horizontal stretch factor 2, vertical stretch factor 2, reflect in the x-axis.
  • c Shift right \pi/2, vertical stretch 3, shift up 2.

a y=3\sin 3x

b y=-2\sin\tfrac12 x

c y=3\sin(x-\pi/2)+2

9 · Sequence from cos x Development

Write a sequence from y=\cos x, sketch for -\pi\le x\le\pi.

a y=5\cos\tfrac12 x · b y=-2\cos 2x-2 · c y=\cos 2\!\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)

  • a Horizontal stretch factor 2, then vertical stretch factor 5.
  • b Horizontal stretch factor 1/2, vertical stretch 2, reflect in x-axis, shift down 2.
  • c Horizontal stretch factor 1/2, then shift right \pi/2.

11 · Solve with diagrams Development

Solve for 0\le x\le 2\pi. Indicate solutions on a sketch of both sides.

a 2\sin(x-\pi/3)=1 · b 2\cos 2x=-1

  • a \sin(x-\pi/3)=\tfrac12 → x=\pi/2 or 7\pi/6.
  • b \cos 2x=-\tfrac12 → x=\pi/3,\,2\pi/3,\,4\pi/3,\,5\pi/3.

12 · Approximate solutions Development

Solve for 0\le x\le\pi, giving answers to 3 d.p.

a \cos(x+0.2)=-0.3 · b \tan 2x=0.5

  • a x=\cos^{-1}(-0.3)-0.2\approx 1.675 (principal value in the domain).
  • b 2x=\tan^{-1}(0.5) or \pi+\tan^{-1}(0.5) → x\approx 0.232 or 1.803.

18 · Intersections with a line Challenge

a Sketch y=2\cos x for -2\pi\le x\le 2\pi.

b On the same diagram sketch y=1-\tfrac12 x.

c How many solutions does 2\cos x=1-\tfrac12 x have?

d Mark P for the negative solution.

e Prove that any solution n satisfies -2\le n\le 6.

  • c Three solutions.
  • d P is the left-hand intersection (negative x).
  • e Since -1\le\cos x\le 1, -2\le 2\cos x\le 2. If equal to 1-\tfrac12 x, then -2\le 1-\tfrac12 x\le 2\Rightarrow -2\le x\le 6.

a–d Same axes; P = negative intersection

y=2\cos xy=1-\tfrac12 x

19 · Exactly one intersection Challenge

a Period of y=\sin(\pi x/2)?

b Sketch y=1+\sin(\pi x/2) for 0\le x\le 4.

c Through what fixed point does y=mx always pass?

d Find m so that \sin(\pi x/2)=mx-1 has exactly one solution in 0\le x\le 4.

  • a Period 4
  • c The origin
  • d m=0 (horizontal line through the trough at x=3), or m>1/4 (line steep enough to clear (4,1) without a second cut)

b y=1+\sin(\pi x/2) on [0,4]
