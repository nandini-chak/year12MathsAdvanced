Notes to copy

Combined sine / cosine form

y=A\sin\big(n(x-b)\big)+c
  • |A| = amplitude; c = mean position.
  • Period =2\pi/|n|.
  • Order: dilations / reflections first, then translations.
  • Always factor: \sin(3x+\pi/2)=\sin\big(3(x+\pi/6)\big), so the shift is -\pi/6 (left), not “π/2 left”.

Modelling checklist

  • Amplitude = distance from mean to crest.
  • Period = time for one full cycle.
  • Mean = centre line of the oscillation.
  • Read t=0 carefully (starting position).

Modelling mini-example (board)

A spring: x=-6\cos(\pi t), with x in cm and t in seconds.

  • At t=0: x=-6 (6 cm below the origin).
  • Amplitude 6 cm; period 2\pi/\pi=2 s.
  • Distance between extremes: 12 cm.
